Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Your plan is stored on your phone.

Does the app work without an internet connection?

Yes. Building your plan, updating your list, scanning, expiry reminders and your communication plan all work offline.

Three things need a connection: finding stores near you, looking up a product name from a barcode, and searching the web for local services.

How do I back up my plan or move it to a new phone?

iPhone: turn on iCloud sync in the app's Settings. Your plan is then kept in your own private iCloud storage and appears on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

If iCloud sync is off, your plan is included in a standard iPhone backup.

Android: turn on "Include my plan in Google backup" in the app's Settings and choose a password. Your plan, medication list and label photos are encrypted on your phone and included in that phone's Google backup. When you set up a new phone from that backup, Prep4Me asks for the password and restores your plan. A forgotten password cannot be recovered.

On either phone you can also export the plan as a PDF at any time.

How do I change my household, time frame or budget?

Open Settings in the app and choose Edit household. Quantities recalculate. Items you already marked and the amounts you have on hand are kept.

The camera did not read an expiration date correctly.

Stamped and embossed dates are hard to read. The app only suggests a date and always asks you to confirm it. Tap the date to correct it with the date picker.

I declined a permission. Can I still use the app?

Yes. Every permission is optional. Without the camera you can type items in. Without notifications, expiring items still appear on the home screen. Without location you can enter a ZIP code or town.

To change a permission later, open your phone's Settings app and choose Prep4Me.

Where do the recommended quantities come from?

From public guidance by Ready.gov and the American Red Cross. That guidance covers roughly the first two weeks. For longer time frames the app scales the numbers and labels them as estimates.

Does Prep4Me recommend or get paid by any store?

No. Nearby stores come from Apple Maps on iPhone and Google Maps on Android. No business is endorsed, sponsored or ranked for pay, and the app contains no affiliate links.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. If you used iCloud sync, also remove the app's data in iPhone Settings, under your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage.

On Android, if you turned on Google backup, also remove it in your Google Account's backup settings. We hold no copy of your plan, so there is nothing for us to delete.

How do I get a refund?

iPhone purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Android purchases are handled by Google Play. Request a refund from your order history in Google Play.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026. Applies to the Prep4Me app for iPhone and Android and to this website.

The short version: the app does not collect your data. Your plan stays on your phone. On iPhone it is also kept in your own iCloud if you turn sync on; on Android it is included in your own Google backup only if you turn that on, and it is encrypted with a password you choose before it leaves your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only personal information we ever receive is what you choose to send us through the contact form, the beta tester and feedback forms, or by email.

Who we are

Prep4Me is a product of B-Tuff LLC ("we", "us"), which publishes the Prep4Me app. Questions about this policy go to admin@prep4me.app.

Information the app collects

None. The app has no account system and does not send your plan, your household details, your contacts, your medication list, your photos or your location to us. We run no servers that receive data from the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores what you enter so it can work: household and pet counts, optional names, home features, your supply list with quantities and expiry dates, saved businesses, your communication plan including the contacts and phone numbers you add, and your medication list, including any photos of medication labels you take.

This information stays on your phone.

iPhone: if you turn on iCloud sync, it is also stored in your private iCloud storage, which is operated by Apple under your Apple Account. We cannot read it.

Android: there is no sync. Your plan is not backed up unless you turn on "Include my plan in Google backup" in the app's Settings. If you do, Prep4Me encrypts your plan, medication list and label photos on your phone with a password you choose before Android copies them to your Google Account. B-Tuff LLC never receives the backup, and neither B-Tuff LLC nor Google can read it. A forgotten password cannot be recovered.

When information leaves your device

Only when you use one of these features:

  • Find nearby. Your search and your location, or the ZIP code or town you type, go to Apple Maps on iPhone, and Apple's privacy policy applies. On Android, Prep4Me opens Google Maps with the store type and the ZIP code or town you typed, and Google's privacy policy applies there.
  • Search for services. This opens a Google search in your web browser. Google's privacy policy applies once you leave the app.
  • Sharing and export. When you email, print or share your plan, it goes where you send it, through the app you pick.
  • iCloud sync (iPhone) or Google backup (Android). If you turn either on, your plan is stored with Apple or Google as described above.

Health information

The medication list is information you type. It is never sent to B-Tuff LLC or anyone else. It leaves your phone only if you share the list yourself, or as part of the iCloud sync or Google backup you turned on. Prep4Me does not give medical advice.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking software. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

Apple and Google may give us anonymous, combined statistics about downloads and crashes from people who have agreed to share them with developers. These do not identify you.

Other people's information

Your communication plan may contain names and phone numbers of family, friends and neighbors. That information is stored the same way as the rest of your plan and is never sent to us. Please share exported plans only with people who should have those details.

Children

Prep4Me is intended for adults planning for their household. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them or from anyone else.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Removing the app erases everything it stored on your phone. B-Tuff LLC holds no copy. iCloud data can be removed in iPhone Settings under iCloud storage. A Google backup, if you turned it on, can be removed in your Google Account's backup settings.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and time of visit, for security.

If you contact us

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, phone number and message. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

We use this information only to answer you. We do not add you to any mailing list, we do not call or text you for marketing, and we do not sell or share it. Messages are kept in our email inbox until the matter is closed and are deleted on request.

Our beta tester and feedback form is delivered by Web3Forms, a form-to-email service, and arrives in our email inbox. It records what you enter: your name, your email address, your phone type and model, and any message. We use this only to invite you to test and to respond to you. Web3Forms' privacy policy applies to the form delivery. Responses are deleted on request.
